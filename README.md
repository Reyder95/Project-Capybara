Bosses

1 - The Hall Guardian - Flies up and flaps wings causing dangerous wind waves, downward beak beam that moves from left to right and vice versa, head spin bullet hell projectiles, floor slams trying to catch Red   

2 - Shadow Red - All of Red’s spells, Speed Boost back and forth, drops items that he can use but Red could also steal them if he’s fast enough 
 
3 - Torol-Sygnis - All Keeper ranged powers (randomized), portals that send Red to previous levels (also randomized) where Torol invisibly prepares a surprise sword strike, blasts that ricochet off geometry and split into smaller shrapnel-like segments, grabs and squeezes Red, follows and streams blood down on Red, casts a magical sword that can slash / launch waves, clones himself, slows time

4 - The Author - Avoidable blasts, beams, and black holes that can temporarily trap Red
